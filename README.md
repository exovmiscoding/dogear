# dogear

a calendar for your desktop 

# Wallpaper

Covers your whole screen and sits behind all your windows, so it doesn’t jump in front when you click it.
Built to stay put when you press Win+D (show desktop).
Alt+F4 won’t close it by accident.
Leaves room for the taskbar so nothing hides behind it.
Starts with Windows by default, from its own copy in AppData, so moving or deleting the Downloads copy doesn’t break it.
Only one copy runs at a time.

# Clock

Big italic time with the date written out, like “saturday, the 14th of november”.
Click the time to switch between 12-hour and 24-hour.
Shows the week number, the day of the year and how many days are left in the year.

# Calendar

Today is circled in red pen.
Flip months with the arrows or the mouse wheel. “today” jumps back to the current month.
Click any day to write a note on the lined slip. It saves by itself.
Days with notes get a little pen squiggle.

# The drawer (your programs)

Opens any program, shortcut, website or app link, like steam://, spotify: or Windows Settings.
“pick a program from my pc…” lets you choose from your Start Menu and fills in the name for you.
“edit” lets you add or remove cards.
Holds 8 programs per page. With more, you get a page counter, dots and mouse-wheel flipping, and extra paper sheets peek out from behind it.
“uninstall” opens Windows’ Installed apps page.

# Look

Three color themes: oat (cream), moss (dark green) and ink (navy). Hover near the clock to switch.

# Animations

On launch, the papers drop onto the desk, the cards deal in, the tape presses down and a pen circles today.
Months flip like pages, and the clock digits roll in when the minute changes.
New cards drop in like they’re being pinned, and deleted ones fall away.
Theme changes fade, and the “add something” form slides up.
Skipped if Windows has “reduce animations” turned on.

Settings (hover near the clock)

Theme swatches, the “starts with windows” switch, and quit.
